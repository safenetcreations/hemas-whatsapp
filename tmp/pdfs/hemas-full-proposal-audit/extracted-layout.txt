  1. Executive Summary & Strategic Objective
Hemas Hospitals is positioned as one of Sri Lanka’s premier healthcare networks. To elevate patient experience,
reduce call-center bottlenecking, and streamline appointment channelling, Safe Net Creations proposes an
enterprise-grade, Meta-certified WhatsApp Automation and AI Engine.

This solution transforms WhatsApp into a 24/7 digital front desk for Hemas Hospitals—enabling automated doctor
appointments, lab report delivery, FAQs, and instant customer service. Recognizing enterprise deployment
dynamics, our proposal supports a phased rollout strategy: starting with a single branch or single core service
(e.g., Doctor Channelling at Wattala/Thalawathugoda) and seamlessly scaling network-wide across all locations and
medical departments.


      Key Strategic Benefits for Hemas Hospitals
       • Zero Patient Friction: Patients book appointments, check doctor availability, and request reports inside
        WhatsApp without downloading external apps.
       • Operational Cost Reduction: Automated AI handles up to 80% of repetitive incoming inquiries (branch
        timing, test costs, doctor schedules), freeing call center agents for complex clinical inquiries.
       • Modular Risk-Free Rollout: Start with 1 branch / 1 service module, validate ROI, and expand seamlessly
        to all hospital facilities.

  3. Phased Implementation & Scale-Up Strategy
To ensure smooth operational adoption without disrupting hospital workflow, we recommend a 3-phase deployment
approach:


   PHASE 1: Pilot Launch (Single Branch / Core Service)
   • Target Focus: Doctor Appointment Channelling & FAQ Bot for 1 Primary Branch (e.g., Hemas Wattala or Thalawathugoda).
   • Deliverables: Meta WhatsApp API setup, basic AI chatbot for doctor schedules, multi-agent inbox for 5–10 front desk staff, and
   staff training.
   • Goal: Test patient adoption, optimize conversation flows, and establish baseline performance metrics.
